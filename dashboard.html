Status rules and data basis

A KPI is On track if it meets or beats its target, At risk if it misses by up to 10% of the target, and Off track if it misses by more than 10%. Lower values are better for loan approval time.

Overall health is Green if all KPIs are on track, Red if two or more are off track, and Amber otherwise.

Y4 Q2 means Year 4, Quarter 2 of the proposed roadmap. This review includes earlier initiatives. All current values and seven targets are illustrative. The 24-hour loan target comes from the team roadmap.

DBS-reported historical context

DBS shifted from 85% outsourced technology staffing in 2014 to 90% in-house in 2019 (2019 annual report). It established 33 platforms in 2018 (2018 annual report).

DBS reported more than 300 customer journeys underway (2016 annual report). In its first year, digibank India reached 1 million customers (May 2017 briefing). More than 50 companies had joined the API platform at its November 2017 launch.

DBS officially launched digibank Indonesia in August 2017. The proposed roadmap places it in Run.
