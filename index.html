Who this is for

The Chief Transformation Officer of a U.S. regional bank with roughly $20–50 billion in assets, who has been asked to present a three-year digital plan to the board and wants to use DBS, one of the most documented bank transformations, as a benchmark for what to build first and how to govern it.

What it lets you do

  • Roadmap: see what slips if a foundation slips. The dependency table shows that a delay in Cloud-native Infrastructure moves both digibank India and the API Developer Platform, so you know which launch dates to warn the board about first.
  • RACI: know who breaks a tie before the conflict starts. When a platform's business and technology co-leads disagree, one row names a single Accountable owner, and another names who decides when a digibank market can proceed to the next one.
  • Dashboard: find the one red signal in ten minutes. The quarterly view puts the off-track loan approval time next to the decision it requires and the person Accountable for making it.

What we added

Our roadmap follows dependencies, not DBS's calendar. We placed the ADA Data Platform in Crawl even though DBS has published no launch date for it, and the comparison table on the roadmap page shows every place where our order differs from what DBS actually did. Two of us have spent our careers in data governance and data integrity, so we treated data as a foundation rather than a later add-on, and the RACI gives data quality and access policy a single Accountable owner, the Chief Data Officer. Every figure on the roadmap is tagged as either DBS-reported or a team-set target, so a reader can tell evidence from assumption. The RACI adds two decisions specific to DBS: when a digibank market proceeds to the next one, and who settles disputes between a platform's business and technology co-leads. On the dashboard, we measure the Platform Operating Model by whether co-leads jointly reviewed their KPIs, not by how many platforms exist, and we publish the exact rules that turn each metric amber or red.

Sources are cited on each page. Case facts come from DBS annual reports, DBS Investor Day 2017, DBS newsroom releases and the MIT CISR case on DBS.
