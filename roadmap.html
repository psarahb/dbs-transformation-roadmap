Phase rationale

Crawl: Foundation (Y1 Q1 – Y1 Q2)

These initiatives establish the "tech stack" and "talent stack" required for all subsequent scaling. We purposefully resequenced the ADA Data Platform to start alongside Cloud-native Infrastructure; while ADA is built on the cloud, their design and governance phases are independent workstreams that can run in parallel. By insourcing talent simultaneously, we ensure that the infrastructure being built is supported by an internal team capable of maintaining it. This foundational phase is essential because without these core capabilities, the bank would remain locked in a cycle of vendor dependency, preventing the agility required for mobile-first deployments.

Walk: Deployment (Y1 Q3 – Y2 Q2)

These initiatives deploy foundational capabilities in limited contexts to prove efficacy. Customer Journey Thinking is piloted in one unit, Retail Lending, using design thinking to shorten loan approval times. digibank India serves as the primary production test case, proving that the insourced team can deliver a mobile-only bank. The API Developer Platform tests our ability to expose services to the Singapore ecosystem. Proving that data-backed experiments and mobile-only banking work in one unit and one market justifies the cost of scaling them across the enterprise.

Run: Scale (Y2 Q3 – Y6 Q4)

These initiatives scale the bank's digital model across the bank and into new markets. The Platform Operating Model moves the bank to "two-in-a-box" co-leadership, where business and technology heads share platform KPIs. It requires the maturity of the Walk phase, because it assumes the bank has mastered cloud-native agility. digibank Indonesia scales the India model into a larger, more complex market through a dedicated regional digital-bank team. This phase embeds digital-native behavior into the bank's culture, so the technology transformation is sustained by a permanent shift in organizational incentives.
